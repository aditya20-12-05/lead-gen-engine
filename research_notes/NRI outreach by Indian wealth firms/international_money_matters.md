## 1. Entity details: city, founder, year, size, clients, AUM, NRI share

### Takeaway
IMMPL is a Bengaluru financial planning and advisory firm founded by Lovaii Navlakhi (operations from November 2001, company incorporated 2002). A TVS Capital-linked investor group took 52% in October 2016. The last hard client figures are from 2016: about 500 fee-paying clients, about 100 of them NRIs (roughly 20%), and Rs 850 crore AUM. A later self-description claims Rs 1,100+ crore of assets, but it is undated.

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: The 2016 vs 2018 conflict may mean the RIA registration was re-issued or converted, for example after a change in control (the 52% stake sale) or a regulatory change. The SEBI register would settle this.
- INFERENCE (RIA/MFD segregation): The RIA number and ARN-30846 appear side by side on one website. A distribution business under the separately linked TVS Wealth Pvt Ltd could not be confirmed. Under the SEBI IA (Amendment) Regulations 2020, a non-individual RIA may offer distribution only with client-level segregation at group level, meaning one client cannot get both advisory and distribution. IMMPL most likely relies on that client-level segregation model, but this was not confirmed. SELEQT runs RIA and MFD side by side, so it should check how IMMPL documents this before copying the model.
- INFERENCE: No overseas entity was found, so IMMPL appears to serve NRIs entirely from India, with offshore expertise coming through partnerships (see section 4).

## 4. Countries targeted and handling of foreign regulation

### Takeaway
Clients are reported across the US, UK, Middle East, South East Asia and Africa. Historically, the firm handled foreign rules by tying up with foreign financial planners for local expertise rather than holding foreign licences. No public US/Canada restriction policy or client-initiated-only disclaimer could be verified.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: Client geography is given as the US, UK, Middle East, South East Asia and Africa. [LinkedIn company page](https://in.linkedin.com/company/international-money-matters-pvt-ltd); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/immpl/__f0gSxKn7BFTxsN2ufsNJGJe2mOnZkPROziD3G3QdtzQ)
- VERIFIED (about 2010 to 2011): "tie-ups with foreign financial planners" to provide local-market expertise for NRI clients. [Cafemutual](https://cafemutual.com/news/tarraki/74-from-dotcom-bust-to-a-booming-practice)
- VERIFIED: Services include "cross-border taxation services" and "asset transfers". [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/immpl/__f0gSxKn7BFTxsN2ufsNJGJe2mOnZkPROziD3G3QdtzQ)
- VERIFIED (negative): No search found an IMMPL tie-up with a named Dubai, Singapore or Middle East firm. [search returned only IMMPL services page](https://www.immpl.com/services/financial-planning/)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: Advising US-person clients from India raises SEC/state investment-adviser registration questions. Partnering with US-licensed planners and keeping the India-side advice limited to Indian assets is the usual mitigation, and it fits the "tie-ups" description. This is not confirmed as IMMPL's current practice.
- INFERENCE: The relocation and returning-to-India focus partly sidesteps foreign rules. Advice delivered while the client is in India, or about Indian assets and RNOR transition, carries less offshore solicitation risk than marketing foreign-domiciled investments.
