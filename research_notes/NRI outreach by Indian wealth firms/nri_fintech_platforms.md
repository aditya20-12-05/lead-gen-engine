## Q1. Acquisition channels: what these platforms actually use

### Takeaway
The proven NRI acquisition engine is **search content plus programmatic SEO on NRI pain points**: GIFT City tax, NRE/NRO, US/Canada fund eligibility, FD rate comparisons, and "best platform for NRIs" listicles. On top of that sit **cash-incentive referral loops**, **PR in Gulf media**, **sponsored "brand studio" articles in Indian media**, and **invite-only NRI summits in Dubai**. I found no public evidence that the platforms studied run influencer programmes at scale. That fits SEBI's 2024 ban on regulated entities associating with unregistered finfluencers.

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: For a small RIA/MFD, the replicable core is (a) deep, jurisdiction-specific content (UAE, US, UK, Singapore) on tax, residency status (RNOR, DTAA), repatriation and US/Canada fund eligibility, and (b) rate and eligibility comparison tools. These cost little, carry no foreign marketing licence risk if they stay educational and are hosted in India, and match exactly what Belong and SBNRI use to scale.
- INFERENCE: Cash referral bonuses like Belong's USD 50 are a broker/platform tactic. A SEBI RIA cannot pay or receive referral fees in the way a distributor can (see Q3), so SELEQT's referral engine has to rely on client goodwill and introducer relationships structured within SEBI rules.
- INFERENCE: The Dubai invite-only summit format, with AMC CEOs as headline speakers, is the closest analogue to what a Rs 1 crore-ticket firm needs. The audience is HNI NRIs, and speakers lend credibility.
- INFERENCE: Most platforms show no paid geo-targeted ad activity in public sources. It may exist, but it cannot be confirmed without ad-library checks (Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency Center).

## Q2. Products used as the NRI hook and the licences behind them

### Takeaway
New entrants lead with **GIFT City (IFSC) USD products**: USD FDs at bank IBUs, the first inbound retail MF, AIFs and GIFT Nifty F&O. These sit under **IFSCA** licences (broker-dealer, PSP, global access provider). They avoid INR conversion, NRE/NRO account friction and Indian tax on qualifying products. Incumbents lead with **SEBI/AMFI rupee mutual funds via NRE/NRO**, plus US stocks through IFSC or US broker-dealer structures.

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: The GIFT City USD hook is strongest with Gulf NRIs, who earn in USD-pegged currencies and have no home-country tax, and with retail-to-mass-affluent tickets. SELEQT is SEBI RIA + AMFI MFD, not IFSCA-licensed. It can advise on and help clients reach GIFT products through IFSCA-licensed partners, but cannot be the platform itself. That is a differentiator only if SELEQT is genuinely product-neutral.
- INFERENCE: Firms serving HNI NRIs (Nuvama, ASK) chose a DFSA licence rather than marketing into the UAE from India. That signals that soliciting Gulf HNIs on the ground needs a local licence.

## Q3. Handling foreign-jurisdiction and Indian rules: disclaimers, US/Canada, geo-blocking, enforcement

### Takeaway
The industry's main compliance pattern is **US/Canada exclusion or restriction**: fund-level flags, AMC declarations, and platforms refusing US/Canada onboarding. It sits alongside **"not solicited abroad" declarations** for those who do accept US/Canada NRIs. In India, SEBI (2024) bars regulated entities from any association with unregistered finfluencers, and NRI advice squarely needs SEBI IA registration. Abroad, the UAE now licenses finfluencers (SCA, May 2025) and regularly warns against unlicensed promoters. The UK FCA asserts reach over any promotion "capable of having effect" in the UK, and says a T&C disclaimer alone is not enough. I found **no public enforcement by DFSA, SCA, MAS or FCA against an Indian NRI investment platform**.

### Cited Findings
**US/Canada handling**
- Many AMCs stopped accepting US/Canada NRI money after FATCA. Kuvera's 2026 guide says "several asset management companies still restrict investments from US and Canada-based NRIs… Only specific AMCs accept investments." — [Kuvera](https://kuvera.in/blog/nri-mutual-fund-investments/); [Cafemutual/Mint 2022](https://cafemutual.com/news/press-news/23693-heres-all-nris-should-know-while-investing-in-mfs-in-india)
- AMC US/Canada NRI declaration forms (Taurus, Oct 2025; Bajaj, 2023; DSP) require the investor to confirm the transaction was submitted **while physically present in India**. The AMC states it **did not market or solicit** the investment abroad and that schemes are not registered with any foreign regulator. — [Taurus declaration](https://taurusmutualfund.com/sites/default/files/2025-10/Declaration_from_NRIs_PIOs_Based_in_USA_Canada.pdf); [Bajaj AMC form](https://www.bajajamc.com/sites/default/files/2023-07/US%20NRI%20Declaration%20Form.pdf); [DSP form](https://dspim.com/media/pages/additional-information/0f457a6df5-1720430499/us-nri-declaration.pdf)
- SBNRI flags US/Canada eligibility on every fund page ("excluding U.S.A. & Canada"). Scripbox refuses new US/Canada residents. Scripbox's earlier US-equity plan was "non-US NRIs" only. — [SBNRI fund page](https://sbnri.com/p/mf/groww-multicap-fund-growth-for-nri); [Scripbox help](https://help.scripbox.com/support/solutions/articles/3000121408-can-nris-open-a-mutual-funds-investment-account-with-scripbox-)
- SEBI KYC currently requires a geo-tag showing the client is in India, even for digital onboarding/V-CIP. An Oct 2025 SEBI proposal would let NRIs do re-KYC/modification from abroad if GPS matches the address-proof country. Adoption not confirmed. — [Cafemutual](https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/36093-soon-nris-may-not-be-required-to-be-physically-present-in-india-for-re-kyc-or-kyc-modification)

**Indian rules (SEBI)**
- SEBI IA Regulations: advice to clients exclusively based outside India is exempt from registration, **except advice to NRIs/PIOs, which falls under IA rules**. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/person-giving-investment-advice-to-overseas-clients-exempted-from-registration-under-ia-rules-sebi-news-309086); [Nishith Desai](https://nishithdesai.com/research-and-articles/hotline/corpsec-hotline/investment-advisers-time-to-register-8408)
- SEBI finfluencer rules: regulated entities and their agents may not associate "directly or indirectly" with any person giving advice or performance claims without SEBI permission. This covers money, client referrals and IT integrations. In force **29 Aug 2024** (Board decision 27 Jun 2024; further tightening Jan 2025). Investor-education content is carved out, as are "specified digital platforms" with safeguards. Breach risks penalties or suspension/cancellation of registration. Note: one source calls the Aug 2024 item a consultation paper, so the dates are reported inconsistently. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/sebi-directs-regulated-entities-to-sever-ties-with-finfluencers-in-3-months); [Business Today Aug 2024](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/investment/story/sebi-tweaks-regulations-for-finfluencers-removes-content-of-over-15000-unregulated-entities-443789-2024-08-31); [Business Standard Jan 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/sebi-finfluencer-circular-live-stock-data-market-education-rules-125013000571_1.html); [Nishith Desai](https://nishithdesai.com/research-and-articles/hotline/technology-law-analysis/securities-market-regulators-continued-quest-against-unfiltered-financial-advice-15193)
- SEBI enforcement on unregistered advice: Avadhut Sathe (Dec 2025 interim order, ₹546.16 cr disgorgement), "Baap of Chart" (2023), Asmita Patel (Feb 2025). — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/avadhut-sathe-finfluencer-sebi-ban-disgorgement-unregistered-advisory-125120500178_1.html); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/market-commentary/story/sebi-order-against-baap-of-chart-fresh-warning-for-unregistered-finfluencers-403344-2023-10-26)
- SEBI Advertisement Code for IAs/RAs (effective 1 May 2023): covers any communication that may influence an investment decision. It requires name, registration number, logo, address and contact details to be shown prominently, plus a disclaimer that returns cannot be assured. RIAs cannot charge referral fees from clients. — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/news/sebi-issues-ad-guidelines-for-registered-investment-advisers-research-analysts-to-prevent-misinformation--news-276215); [Cafemutual on return assurance](https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/28925-rias-cannot-provide-return-assurance-to-investors-sebi); [Cafemutual on referral fees](https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/22324-rias-cannot-charge-referral-fees-from-clients-sebi)

**Foreign regulators**
- **UAE SCA finfluencer licence**: Resolution No. 10/R.M of 2025, in force from **28 May 2025**. It covers financial recommendations on social media, blogs and webinars. Licensees must disclose their SCA number and paid promotions. Fees were waived for three years, and penalties of up to AED 200,000 are reported (unconfirmed). DIFC/ADGM are outside it but subject to DFSA/FSRA promotion rules. One source says new registrations from 2026 go to the CMA (single source). — [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/uae-new-licensing-regime-finfluencers); [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/united-arab-emirates/media-telecoms-it-entertainment/how-to-finfluence-without-getting-fined-a-glimpse-into-uae's-finfluencer-regulations); [Complyport](https://complyport.com/are-you-a-finfluencer-looking-to-give-trading-advice-the-uae-says-not-without-a-licence/); [UpperSetup](https://uppersetup.com/en/blog/the-uae-finfluencer-license-2026-registering-financial)
- UAE warnings against unlicensed promoters are frequent. The SCA "observed that some entities advertise and promote financial services… without obtaining the necessary licences" (Better Experience). It warned against Top Profit Marketing and an unlicensed Instagram influencer. The CMA warned against Arbitrage Prim (May 2026). None involved Indian NRI platforms. — [Khaleej Times Better Experience](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/uae-sca-warns-investors-unlicensed-better-experience); [Gulf News Top Profit](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/sca-warns-investors-against-unlicensed-top-profit-marketing-firm-instagram-influencer-borajb-1.500272946); [Khaleej Times Arbitrage Prim](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/cma-issue-warning-unlicensed-firm-arbitrage-prim)
- **UK FCA**: in the HTX case (proceedings 21 Oct 2025), the FCA treated a site accessible to UK consumers "with no apparent blocks or controls" as breaching s.21 FSMA. Commentary says the regime covers communications "capable of having effect in the UK" wherever the communicator is based, and that a T&C exclusion is not protection if UK users can still access the platform. The case concerned crypto, but s.21 applies to all financial promotions. — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/global-cryptoexchange-sued-by-uk-regulator-for-breaching-financial-promotions-rules); [Withers](https://www.withersworldwide.com/en-gb/insight/read/crypto-exchanges-a-warning-to-overseas-firms-promoting-to-uk-customers)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE (lawful-playbook implications for SELEQT):
  1. Advising NRIs requires SEBI RIA registration, which SELEQT holds.
  2. Never pay or partner with unregistered creators, including Gulf/US NRI creators; co-hosting may count as "association".
  3. Every ad, post and webinar must carry the SEBI Ad Code disclosures.
  4. Keep content educational and India-hosted rather than solicitation targeted into a jurisdiction.
  5. For US/Canada prospects, expect AMC restrictions and declarations, and avoid active solicitation. Paid geo-targeted ads to US residents are the riskiest channel (Advisers Act implications are unresearched here).
  6. For UK and UAE, geo-targeted paid promotion by an unlicensed foreign firm carries real regulatory risk. Organic content and inbound enquiries are lower risk. An in-person Dubai event needs a local legal check; firms like Nuvama and ASK took DFSA licences.
- INFERENCE: The absence of foreign enforcement against Indian NRI platforms may reflect their reliance on inbound/organic and educational content, plus US/Canada exclusions. It does not show that paid foreign solicitation is permitted.
