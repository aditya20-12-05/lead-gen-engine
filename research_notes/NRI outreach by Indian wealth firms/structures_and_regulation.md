# Structures and regulatory boundaries for Indian wealth/advisory/distribution firms serving NRIs abroad

Prepared 11 Oct 2026 for SELEQT Private Wealth (Ahmedabad; SEBI RIA + AMFI MFD). Research method note: WebFetch and curl to all target hosts (sebi.gov.in, ifsca.gov.in, dfsa.ae, hubbis.com, khaleejtimes.com etc.) failed with DNS/proxy errors in this environment, so every finding below comes from search-engine result snippets/summaries, not from reading the full page. Each claim is tagged VERIFIED (snippet from the cited URL says it) or INFERENCE (my reasoning). Snippet-based facts should be spot-checked on the primary page before external use. Nothing here is legal advice.

## 1. Offices and licences abroad: what each structure allows, cost/capital, and which Indian firms hold them

### Takeaway
No single structure lets a small Indian RIA/MFD lawfully advise or solicit NRIs across the Gulf, Singapore, UK and EU. Each host country licenses activity carried out "in or from" its territory, and a licence in one zone does not travel: a DFSA licence covers DIFC, not onshore UAE or ADGM. The cheapest real footholds are (a) a DFSA Representative Office (marketing only, about USD 6k application fee, no advice or arranging) and (b) a DFSA/FSRA Category 4 advising-and-arranging licence (base capital about USD 10k to 50k, depending on source and year, plus substance costs). Larger Indian firms (360 ONE, Nuvama, ASK, Motilal Oswal, Spark, Kotak) use full DIFC licences. Geojit has used Gulf JVs and bank partnerships for 15 to 20 years.

### Cited Findings

**DIFC / DFSA: Representative Office**
- VERIFIED: The DFSA rulebook says operating a Representative Office is the least regulated form of Financial Service. It is open only to a firm regulated elsewhere that limits itself to marketing. Permitted marketing includes making introductions and distributing research or prospectuses. A Representative Office "may not make arrangements in regard to credit, investments or insurance, or provide advice in regard to them" — [DFSA Rulebook: Representative Office](https://dfsaen.thomsonreuters.com/rulebook/representative-office); [RIAA Barker Gillette summary](https://riaabarkergillette.com/uae/insight-article/difc-representative-office-regulated-by-the-dfsa/)
- VERIFIED: The DFSA service page for a new Representative Office authorisation lists a service fee of USD 6,000 — [DFSA services: Rep Office authorisation](https://services.dfsa.ae/authorised-person/authorised-firms-request-for-new-authorisation-representative-offices/). No annual fee for rep offices was found. The DFSA fees page gives USD 15,000 to 70,000 application fees for full authorised firms — [DFSA Fees](https://www.dfsa.ae/your-resources/regulatory/fees)
- VERIFIED: Anand Rathi Wealth "serves non-resident Indian clients through a representative office in Dubai" (licensing regime not specified in snippet) — [India Infoline company summary](https://www.indiainfoline.com/company/anand-rathi-wealth-ltd/summary); [Arihant Capital company info](https://www.arihantcapital.com/company-information/about-company/67729)
- VERIFIED: Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers proposed a wholly owned Dubai subsidiary aimed at NRIs, HNIs and family offices, "subject to approvals from the Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism and SEBI". DET approval implies a mainland/DET entity, not DIFC. Outcome not found — [JM Financial market news](https://www.jmfinancialservices.in/market-news-and-insights/1710245)

**DIFC / DFSA: Category 4 (advising and arranging) and Category 3C (asset management/advisory with custody arrangements)**
- VERIFIED: Category 4 covers arranging deals in investments, advising on financial products, fund administration and similar activities. Firms may not hold or control client money or assets. Base capital is the higher of USD 10,000 or an expenditure-based capital minimum — [Mondaq: DIFC licensing categories](https://www.mondaq.com/fund-management-reits/1720078/difc-licensing-categories-video); [Hadef Partners](https://hadefpartners.com/news-insights/insights/establishment-of-a-dfsa-regulated-entity/)
- VERIFIED (sources conflict): One 2026 consultant says the Category 4 base capital is "US$30,000, up from US$10,000 a few months ago". Another says USD 10k plus an expenditure-based requirement of 6/52 of annual opex. Approval timelines are quoted as 3 to 4 months by one source and 8 to 12 months by another. Category 4 is typically Professional Clients only, with an optional Retail Endorsement — [10 Leaves DIFC Cat 4 guide](https://10leaves.ae/publications/difc/category-4-difc-investment-advisory-license-difc-license-categories); [Zitadelle DFSA Cat 4 2026](https://www.zitadelleag.com/services/investment-licensing/dubai-dfsa-category-4); [Henry Club DFSA categories 2026](https://www.henryclub.ae/business-setup/dfsa-license-categories/). The DFSA is phasing in activity-based prudential reforms over 2025 and 2026, so figures are moving — [Henry Club](https://www.henryclub.ae/business-setup/dfsa-license-categories/)
- VERIFIED: One guide gives a Category 3C base capital of USD 140,000 (dated) — [Neolegal: DIFC & ADGM for Indian fund managers](https://neolegal.ae/insights/difc-adgm-indian-fund-managers-uae-establishment)

**UAE onshore (SCA, renamed CMA from 1 Jan 2026) and DMCC/mainland "consultancy"**
- VERIFIED: The SCA was renamed the Capital Market Authority (CMA) effective 1 Jan 2026 under Federal Decree-Laws No. 32 and 33 of 2025, with wider powers. DIFC and ADGM remain outside its remit — [Regulation Tomorrow, Jan 2026](https://www.regulationtomorrow.com/2026/01/uae-sca-renamed-cma-and-other-landmark-legislations/); [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/major-updates-recalibrate-the-uae-capital-markets-regime)
- VERIFIED: Under SCA Board Decision 3/2017 (Promotion and Introduction), promoting financial products to persons in the UAE requires an SCA licence, which requires an onshore UAE presence. Promotion to institutions and reverse solicitation are excluded. Promotion to retail or HNW individuals is not permitted without a licence — [Clifford Chance 2017](https://financialmarketstoolkit.cliffordchance.com/content/dam/cliffordchance/briefings/2017/02/uae-securities-regulator-creates-regime-for-promotion-and-introduction-to-uae-investors.pdf)
- VERIFIED: After the 2023 amendments, reverse solicitation is available only to promoters outside the UAE acting cross-border. Foreign funds must be promoted through local SCA-licensed promoters. A 2026 guide says retail promotion of foreign funds on the mainland has been closed — [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/bbb77961/sweeping-changes-to-the-sca-rules-on-the-distribution-of-foreign-funds-in-the-uae); [Morgan Lewis 2023](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2023/03/scas-official-guidance-on-marketing-of-foreign-funds-in-mainland-uae-five-key-takeaways); [Kayrouz 2026](https://www.kayrouzandassociates.com/insights/foreign-fund-promotion-uae)
- VERIFIED: A general free-zone (e.g. DMCC) "consultancy" licence is non-regulated. Advisory work may still need SCA/CMA, DFSA or FSRA approval, and assuming otherwise "carries significant compliance risk" — [Global Law Experts 2026](https://globallawexperts.com/how-to-set-up-an-investment-advisory-firm-in-uae-2026/); [Kayrouz: who needs a licence 2026](https://www.kayrouzandassociates.com/insights/who-needs-a-licence-to-give-investment-advice-in-the-uae)
- VERIFIED: Geojit's Gulf model uses JVs and partnerships: Barjeel Geojit Securities (UAE JV with the Al Saud group, branches in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, RAK, Al Ain and Sharjah), BBK Geojit Securities KSC (Kuwait JV with Bank of Bahrain and Kuwait), an NRI desk at BBK Bahrain (2007), and partners in Saudi Arabia and Oman — [Geojit NRI services](https://www.geojit.com/gil/nri-services); [Business Standard 2011](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/geojit-forms-jv-with-bbk-and-jz-associates-111080900175_1.html); [Arab News 2007](https://www.arabnews.com/node/303572); [Business Standard 2012](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/companies/geojit-launches-qfi-investment-service-112062100162_1.html)

**Singapore (MAS)**
- VERIFIED: Under FAA s6(1), anyone acting as a financial adviser in Singapore needs a licence unless exempt, even when marketing to persons outside Singapore if the Singapore nexus exists — [MAS FAA FAQ](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas/regulations-and-financial-stability/regulations-guidance-and-licensing/financial-advisers/faq/faa_faqs_24nov2017.pdf)
- VERIFIED: An FA licence requires a company (MAS generally expects a Singapore-incorporated company for FA business), at least 2 directors with 1 resident, a Singapore-resident CEO with 10+ years' experience, and at least 3 full-time Singapore-based staff with 5+ years' experience — [MAS: Financial Adviser's Licence](https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/capital-markets/apply-for-licensing-or-registration-of-capital-market-entities/financial-advisers); [Corporate Services 2026](https://www.corporateservices.com/singapore/financial-services-licenses-mas-singapore/)

**UK (FCA) and EU**
- VERIFIED: An appointed-representative (AR) model lets an unauthorised firm operate under an FCA-authorised principal. HM Treasury consulted 12 Feb to 9 Apr 2026 on requiring principals to hold a specific new FCA permission, a new AR Senior Management Function, and extended FOS jurisdiction over ARs. The implementation timetable is unclear — [Skadden, Feb 2026](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/02/hm-treasury-consults-on-significant-reforms); [Mayer Brown, Mar 2026](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/reforms-to-the-uk-appointed-representative-regime); [Regulation Tomorrow](https://www.regulationtomorrow.com/2026/02/hmt-consults-on-changes-to-the-appointed-representatives-regime/)
- VERIFIED: UK Financial Promotion Order Art. 31 exempts non-real-time promotions sent by an overseas communicator from outside the UK to a "previously overseas customer" (business done in the last 12 months while the customer was not UK-resident). Art. 32 covers unsolicited real-time calls only with prior expectation and a warning — [legislation.gov.uk FPO art. 31](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1529/article/31/made); [FCA PERG 8](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/perg8/perg8s37)
- VERIFIED: ESMA (Jan 2021) said general T&C clauses or online "I agree" boxes claiming exclusive client initiative do not amount to reverse solicitation. Under MiFID II recital 111, a third-country firm that solicits, promotes or advertises in the EU, or has someone acting on its behalf, cannot rely on reverse solicitation — [ESMA statement ESMA35-43-2509](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/library/esma35-43-2509%5Fstatement%5Fon%5Freverse%5Fsolicitation.pdf); [CMS](https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/esma-observes-questionable-reverse-solicitation-practices-after-brexit)

The Anand Rathi Dubai subsidiary was also subject to SEBI approval — [JM Financial](https://www.jmfinancialservices.in/market-news-and-insights/1710245)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: For a small RIA, the lowest-cost lawful Gulf presence is a DFSA Representative Office. It can introduce clients and distribute materials about SELEQT's India services, but every advice conversation and every transaction arrangement must happen from India under the SEBI registration. Whether a SEBI RIA counts as "regulated elsewhere" for DFSA purposes, and whether a DIFC rep office's marketing may reach onshore UAE residents without a CMA promotion licence, need DFSA/counsel confirmation.
- INFERENCE: The Nuvama transcript and the Vault Wealth case (section 4) together show that serving UAE-resident NRIs at scale onshore may need a CMA (ex-SCA) licence on top of any DIFC/ADGM licence. Indian firms appear to be moving from DIFC-only models to adding onshore licences.
- INFERENCE: The Geojit JV and bank-partner model shows a capital-light alternative: partner with a locally licensed entity (bank, exchange house or broker) that holds the local licence and the client relationship, while the Indian firm provides India-side execution and research. For an RIA, revenue-sharing in such an arrangement must be checked against SEBI IA fee rules and the 2024 association rules.
- INFERENCE: In Singapore, the staffing and residency rules make an own FA licence uneconomic for a small firm. Practical options are partnering with an MAS-licensed FA, serving clients wholly from India on genuine client initiative, or a GIFT IFSC product route.
- INFERENCE: In the UK, an AR arrangement under an FCA principal is the cheapest licensed route, but principals take on liability and the 2026 reforms raise their oversight burden. A small Indian firm would need a principal willing to accept India-asset advice.

## 2. GIFT IFSC as an NRI channel

### Takeaway
GIFT IFSC lets an Indian firm offer NRIs USD-denominated, tax-advantaged products (IBU FDs, feeder funds, AIFs, PMS) without a foreign licence, and IFSCA now has a perpetual distributor registration that even home-regulated foreign distributors can use. However, IFSCA registration governs the product and intermediary inside GIFT. It does not authorise soliciting residents of the UAE, Singapore, UK or EU, where host-country promotion rules still apply.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: The IFSCA (Capital Market Intermediaries) Regulations 2025 replaced the 2021 regulations and folded in the distributor framework (previously in circulars). A registered distributor may distribute capital market products and services to specified clients and investors — [TaxGuru analysis](https://taxguru.in/finance/ifsca-capital-market-intermediaries-regulations-2021-2025-analysis.html); [AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/navigating-distribution-of-capital-market-products-services-in-ifsc/)
- VERIFIED: IFSCA issued a Master Circular for Distributors in the IFSC on 5 Aug 2025. Distributor registration certificates are perpetual unless suspended or cancelled, and there is a simplified application route — [IFSCA Master Circular PDF](https://ifsca.gov.in/CommonDirect/ViewFile?id=21626bde60601ef44a0ed02201680805&fileName=Master_Circular_for_Distributors_in_the_IFSC_20250805_0224.pdf); [Nexpective summary](https://nexpective.in/ifsca-issues-master-circular-for-distributors-in-ifsc-august-2025-update/)
- VERIFIED: Per an IFSCA interpretive letter of 20 Aug 2025 (as reported), a distributor registered in its home jurisdiction without a GIFT presence may distribute GIFT IFSC products if it complies with the CMI Regulations Code of Conduct and manages conflicts. An IFSCA-registered fund management entity needs a separate distributor registration to distribute — [ELP Law](https://elplaw.in/leadership/distribution-of-capital-market-products-in-gift-ifsc-guidance-from-the-ifsca/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/fund-management-reits/1713944/navigating-distribution-of-capital-market-productsservices-in-ifsc)
- VERIFIED: Distributor compliance includes AML/CFT, FIU-IND FINGate registration and quarterly reporting — [Nexpective](https://nexpective.in/ifsca-distributor-registration-gift-city/)
- VERIFIED: The IFSCA (Fund Management) Regulations 2025 were reportedly notified 19 Feb 2025 and amended later. Commentary says NRIs, PIOs, overseas trusts and others are eligible investors, and Indian residents are limited to LRS limits. Minimums are reported as USD 75,000 for PMS and some AIF figures, but sources conflict — [IQ-EQ](https://iqeq.com/insights/gift-city-unlocking-nri-investment-opportunities-through-fund-management-regulations/); [GiftCFO](https://www.giftcfo.com/post/how-nris-can-invest-in-gift-city-a-complete-guide); [Bhatt & Joshi on IFSC PMS](https://bhattandjoshiassociates.com/gift-city-pms-regulations-ifsca-framework-for-discretionary-portfolio-management/)
- VERIFIED: Section 10(4D) of the Income-tax Act exempts non-residents' gains on transfer of units of specified IFSC funds. The benefit is extended to 2030 — [GiftCFO](https://www.giftcfo.com/post/how-nris-can-invest-in-gift-city-a-complete-guide)
- VERIFIED: Axis Bank's GIFT City IBU offers USD FDs to existing NRIs (7 days to 10 years, minimum USD 1,100). IBU deposits carry no DICGC cover — [Axis Bank GIFT City FD](https://www.axis.bank.in/nri/nri-deposits/gift-city-fixed-deposit); [Belong: GIFT MFs vs FCNR](https://getbelong.com/blog/gift-city-mutual-funds-vs-fcnr-deposits/)
- VERIFIED: Tata India Dynamic Equity Fund (GIFT feeder into Indian MFs/ETFs) launched Sep 2025. Sundaram runs an IFSC inbound feeder. The Wealth Company launched a GIFT Category III AIF fund-of-funds on 15 Aug 2026 as a USD route into Indian MFs, explicitly citing investors in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman — [Getbelong GIFT MF list](https://getbelong.com/blog/gift-city-mutual-funds-list/); [Sundaram GIFT City](https://www.sundarammutual.com/gift-city); [PR Newswire, Aug 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-wealth-company-launches-gift-city-fund-to-give-nris-a-single-dollar-denominated-route-into-indias-mutual-fund-market-302851983.html)
- VERIFIED: NRIs/OCIs may now contribute 100% of the corpus of IFSCA-regulated FPIs — [GiftCFO](https://www.giftcfo.com/post/how-nris-can-invest-in-gift-city-a-complete-guide)
- VERIFIED: A Cafemutual Investment Summit in Dubai (20 Oct 2025) included GIFT City sessions for MFDs and wealth managers serving NRIs — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/why-now-is-the-moment-for-gulf-nris-to-bet-big-on-indias-equity-markets?amp=1); [Cafemutual](https://cafemutual.com/news/events/36246-know-how-nris-can-include-india-in-their-global-portfolios)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: SELEQT could register as an IFSCA distributor (possibly through a GIFT branch or entity) and offer GIFT USD feeders, AIFs and IBU FDs to NRIs onboarded from India. This suits Gulf NRIs, who are in zero-tax jurisdictions, and US persons are excluded in practice by PFIC issues. Marketing those products inside the UAE, Singapore, UK or EU is still governed by host rules: for example, UAE CMA promotion rules for foreign funds, and UK s21 FSMA.
- INFERENCE: The Aug 2025 interpretive letter opens a partnership model in reverse. A Gulf-licensed distributor (e.g. a DFSA/FSRA/CMA-licensed firm) can distribute GIFT products under its home licence, so SELEQT could act as the India/GIFT-side manager or adviser while the local partner solicits.
- INFERENCE: An RIA advising on GIFT products and an MFD distributing them must still respect SEBI client-level segregation (section 3) if the same group does both.

### Gaps
- IFSCA primary texts could not be fetched, so the exact definition of "specified clients and investors" for distributors, and any IFSCA marketing/advertising code, are unconfirmed.
- No IFSCA rule was found that expressly addresses solicitation of foreign residents. No authoritative source was found on whether IFSCA expects distributors to hold host-country licences when marketing abroad, beyond the general Code of Conduct.
- No case of IFSCA enforcement on distribution was found.

## 3. SEBI/AMFI rules relevant to marketing to NRIs

### Takeaway
The Indian-side constraints are the IA advertisement code (prior approval by the supervisory body under the Feb 2026 Master Circular), client-level segregation of advice and distribution, the Aug 2024 ban on associating with unregistered finfluencers or anyone making return claims (which also catches paid referral arrangements), AMFI's anti-rebating code, and FATCA-driven AMC limits for US and Canada NRIs. SEBI is moving to enable fully digital overseas KYC for NRIs/OCIs (Aug 2026 consultation), which would remove the main operational friction for remote onboarding.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: The SEBI advertisement code for IAs/RAs (circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2023/51, 5 Apr 2023, effective 1 May 2023) covers all communications, including social media and electronic messaging. Ads must show the registered name, address, SEBI registration number and logo, and a standard risk warning in at least 10-pt font, accurately translated if non-English — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-issues-advertisement-code-for-investment-advisers-research-analysts/); [CA Club circular PDF](https://files.caclub.in/wp-content/uploads/sebi-circular-dated-05-04-2023-ad-code-ia-ra.pdf)
- VERIFIED: A 2026 SEBI consultation paper on a common advertisement code says the IA code now sits in para 10 of the IA Master Circular dated 6 Feb 2026. It requires prior approval of all IA/RA ads by the SEBI-recognised supervisory body. Harmonisation across intermediaries is proposed — [SEBI consultation paper, Jun 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2026/1782210871344.pdf)
- VERIFIED: SEBI amended the Intermediaries, SECC and Depositories regulations on 29 Aug 2024. Regulated persons (including MFDs, IAs, AMCs, PMS and AIF distributors) may not have any direct or indirect association, including monetary transactions, client referrals or tech integration, with persons giving securities advice without registration or making return/performance claims. Exemptions cover pure investor education and "specified digital platforms". Existing contracts had to end within 3 months of the 22 Oct 2024 circular — [Nishith Desai](https://nishithdesai.com/research-and-articles/hotline/technology-law-analysis/securities-market-regulators-continued-quest-against-unfiltered-financial-advice-15193); [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2024/09/223-sebi-regulated-firms-barred-from-collaborating-with-unregistered-finfluencers/); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/sebi-directs-regulated-entities-to-sever-ties-with-finfluencers-in-3-months)
- VERIFIED: The IA Regulations 2020 require client-level segregation of advice and distribution. Individual RIAs cannot distribute. Non-individual RIAs may do both only through separate entities/divisions with group-level client segregation, so no client gets both. Family-of-client and family-of-RIA rules apply (dependent spouse, children and parents) — [Business Standard, Jul 2020](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/finance/under-new-ria-norms-all-your-financial-needs-won-t-be-under-one-roof-120070901037_1.html)
- VERIFIED: The Dec 2024 IA framework revision raised the individual RIA client cap to 300 before corporatisation and relaxed qualifications — [Business Standard, Dec 2024](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/capital-market-news/sebi-introduces-new-guidelines-to-make-registered-investment-advisor-framework-more-accessible-124121700708_1.html)
- VERIFIED: The AMFI Code of Conduct (rev. Apr 2022) prohibits rebating, i.e. passing back any part of commission, gifts or inducements to investors. Breach can lead to ARN suspension or cancellation, and AMCs may not deal with non-compliant intermediaries — [AMFI unified circular](https://amfiindia.com/themes/theme1/downloads/circulars/unifiedamficircular.pdf); [SEBI circular 2002](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/commondocs/cirmfd62002_h.html)
- VERIFIED: AMFI has an overseas distributor registration. SEBI does not require it, but AMFI advises a one-time registration for tracking, with an ARN that does not expire. Applicants must show proof of registration or regulation in the jurisdictions where they distribute and must follow local laws. A 2013 report put the fee at USD 1,000 for corporates and USD 100 for individuals. An NRI can hold an Indian ARN but may only source NRI/foreign clients. An MFD who becomes a permanent resident abroad must surrender the Indian ARN and re-register as an overseas distributor — [AMFI overseas distributor circular](https://www.amfiindia.com/uploads/Circular_Registration_form_for_Overseas_Distributors_4543289cac.pdf); [Business Standard 2013](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/markets/amfi-may-bring-distributors-abroad-under-its-net-113042900628_1.html); [Cafemutual: NRI can be MFD](https://cafemutual.com/news/askus/28325-an-nri-can-become-a-mutual-fund-distributor); [Cafemutual: relocating MFDs](https://cafemutual.com/news/askus/28351-mfds-relocating-to-other-countries-must-surrender-arn)
- VERIFIED: Because of FATCA/CRS, many platforms bar US and Canada NRIs. Only a subset of AMCs accept them, often offline or with physical presence. Lists conflict: Zerodha cites ABSL, SBI, UTI, ICICI Pru, PPFAS, Nippon, Quant and others. Belong says 8 to 10 AMCs. Motilal Oswal notes the ABSL FATCA declaration — [Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/varsity/can-us-canada-nris-invest-in-indian-mutual-funds); [Motilal Oswal, Sep 2025](https://www.motilaloswal.com/learning-centre/2025/9/mutual-fund-houses-that-allow-usa-canada-nris-to-invest-in-india); [Getbelong](https://getbelong.com/blog/mutual-funds/how-nris-can-invest.md)
- VERIFIED: NRIs in the UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, most of continental Europe and the rest of the Gulf face no AMC-level residency restriction — [Dealplexus](https://www.dealplexus.com/blog/nri-mutual-fund-restrictions-kyc)
- VERIFIED: SEBI's 14 Aug 2026 consultation paper proposes fully digital KYC for individual PROIs (NRIs, OCIs and foreign nationals) in FATF-compliant countries without being in India. It proposes e-signature, VIPV, liveness checks and live geo-capture matching the proof-of-address country, plus KYC portability. Comments closed 4 Sep 2026. It follows the Dec 2025 relaxation of geo-tagging for NRI re-KYC and 12 Jun 2026 FEMA amendments — [Business Today, 14 Aug 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/nri/story/nris-ocis-can-complete-sebi-kyc-from-abroad-what-changes-under-new-proposal-549339-2026-08-14); [TeamLease RegTech](https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/58745/sebi-issued-consultation-paper-on-review-of-know-your-client-process-for-individual-persons-resident-outside-india/); [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com/article/175615-indian-market-regulator-sebi-proposes-fully-digital-kyc-for-nris-ocis-foreign-nationals)
- VERIFIED: Cafemutual reported a SEBI call for the MF industry to suggest ways to simplify NRI onboarding — [Cafemutual](https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/32728-mf-industry-should-suggest-ways-to-simplify-onboarding-of-nri-investors-sebi)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: SELEQT's RIA and MFD arms must keep separate NRI client lists. A Gulf NRI receiving advice under the RIA cannot be onboarded as an MFD client of the group, and family-level checks apply.
- INFERENCE: Paying Gulf community influencers, YouTubers or "NRI finance" pages per lead, or co-branding with them, is high-risk under the Aug 2024 association rules unless they are SEBI-registered or doing pure education with no advice or return claims. Paying unregistered referrers a share of RIA fees or MF commission is similarly risky. For MFDs, passing commission to investors is barred rebating, and sharing it with unregistered sub-agents is restricted by the ARN/EUIN regime (not separately verified).
- INFERENCE: Every piece of NRI-facing content (webinars, WhatsApp broadcasts, Gulf newspaper columns) is an "advertisement" under the IA code and needs supervisory-body prior approval plus the standard disclosures, including in translated versions.
- INFERENCE: The AMFI overseas-distributor framework means a Gulf-licensed local partner can itself register with AMFI and earn AMC commission directly. This is the cleanest lawful partnership structure for MF distribution abroad, because SELEQT's ARN never pays an unregistered party.

### Gaps
- The SEBI IA Master Circular (6 Feb 2026) text was not read. Whether it contains NRI-specific or overseas-solicitation obligations for RIAs (for example, compliance with foreign laws) is unconfirmed.
- Whether the "specified digital platform" framework was finalised was not confirmed.
- No SEBI rule or FAQ was found that expressly addresses an RIA soliciting clients physically abroad. SEBI regulates the intermediary, not foreign solicitation.
- The current AMFI overseas-distributor fee and any recent update to the framework (sources are from 2012/2013) were not confirmed.

## 4. Enforcement examples showing the limits

### Takeaway
No public action was found in which a Gulf, Singapore, UK or EU regulator named a SEBI-registered Indian wealth firm for unlicensed solicitation of NRIs. The closest precedent is the DFSA's Oct 2026 fine on ADGM-licensed Vault Wealth for advising and onboarding clients in DIFC: a licence in a neighbouring UAE zone is no defence. The UAE regulator also routinely names unlicensed firms and unlicensed finfluencers, Qatar regulators warn about unlicensed investment promoters, the FCA lists India-addressed firms targeting UK residents, and SEBI regularly bans unregistered advisers.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: On 1 Oct 2026 the DFSA fined Vault Wealth Limited (ADGM-incorporated, FSRA-licensed) AED 401,000 (USD 109,200), reduced 30% from AED 573,000 on settlement. Between Feb and May 2024, VWL staff worked from the DIFC office of a related unregulated company, invited prospective clients there for advice, and collected KYC documents to open investment accounts. This breached the Art. 41(1) Regulatory Law 2004 Financial Services Prohibition. DFSA enforcement head Alan Linning said authorisation by another regulator does not permit services in or from DIFC — [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c2ivgxq-dfsa-fines-vault-wealth-limited-109200-for); [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2026/10/01/dfsa-fines-vault-wealth-dh401000-for-providing-unauthorised-transactions/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/banking/dfsa-fines-vault-wealth-109200-over-unauthorised-services-1.500694971)
- VERIFIED: The DFSA issues alerts on firms falsely claiming DIFC location or authorisation, and on clones of authorised firms — [DFSA alert: false DIFC claim](https://www.dfsa.ae/alerts/company-falsely-claims-be-difc-advanced-fee-scam); [DFSA alert: impersonation](https://www.dfsa.ae/alerts/dfsa-authorised-firm-impersonated-various-websites)
- VERIFIED: The SCA (now CMA) repeatedly warns against unlicensed firms and named individuals, including a social-media trading-channel operator "not registered with the SCA as a licensed finfluencer" and an Instagram influencer — [Khaleej Times: unlicensed finfluencer](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/uae-sca-warning-unlicensed-finfluencer-companies); [Khaleej Times: Thoufeek Raja Abdul Majeeth](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/sca-warns-investors-thoufeek-raja-abdul-majeeth); [Gulf News: Top Profit / borajb](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/sca-warns-investors-against-unlicensed-top-profit-marketing-firm-instagram-influencer-borajb-1.500272946); [Khaleej Times: company without SCA licence](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/uae-authority-warns-residents-of-company-providing-financial-services-without-sca-licence). (Nationality of the individuals was not stated in snippets, so no Indian link is claimed.)
- VERIFIED: The UAE finfluencer licence (launched 2025) requires anyone giving investment recommendations on regulated UAE products via social media, podcasts, webinars or in-person seminars to be licensed. Fees are waived for the first 3 years — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae-finfluencer-licence-sca); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/your-money/saving-investment/finfluencers-in-uae-guidelines-to-follow-before-promoting-financial-products-1.500166583); [Complyport](https://complyport.com/are-you-a-finfluencer-looking-to-give-trading-advice-the-uae-says-not-without-a-licence/)
- VERIFIED: The QFMA (Mar 2023) warned about firms advertising savings/investment profits without licences. Qatar's Ministry of Commerce (Jul 2025) said unlicensed parties cannot lawfully raise funds or offer investment services. The QFCRA flagged unlicensed sites (e.g. AAQ Investment, via IOSCO I-SCAN) and has fined an advisory firm over false claims — [Qatar Tribune](https://qatar-tribune.com/article/54965/business/qfma-warns-investors-against-dealing-with-suspicious-firms); [The Peninsula, Jul 2025](https://s.thepeninsula.qa/article/09/07/2025/moci-warns-against-unlicensed-investment-activities); [IOSCO I-SCAN](https://www.iosco.org/i-scan/?id=34673); [International Adviser](https://international-adviser.com/qatar-financial-regulator-fines-advisory-firm-after-false-claims-investigation)
- VERIFIED: The FCA warning list includes "Momentum Weekend Investing", listed with a Noida, Uttar Pradesh address, as an unauthorised firm "may be targeting people in the UK". It also lists "India Trade Investment" (Stockport address) — [FCA: Momentum Weekend Investing](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/momentum-weekend-investing); [FCA: India Trade Investment](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/india-trade-investment)
- VERIFIED: SEBI (Aug 2025) issued an advisory against fraudulent trading schemes claiming to be offered to Indian residents by FPIs — [SEBI press release](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/aug-2025/advisory-against-investment-in-fraudulent-trading-schemes-claiming-to-be-offered-to-indian-residents-by-fpis_96244.html)

- VERIFIED: ESMA (2021) observed "questionable" reverse-solicitation practices by non-EU firms after Brexit — [ESMA statement](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/library/esma35-43-2509%5Fstatement%5Fon%5Freverse%5Fsolicitation.pdf)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: The Vault Wealth facts (staff meeting prospects in a related unregulated office, KYC collection, account opening) map closely onto the "fly-in advisor holds client meetings in Dubai" pattern used by many Indian RIAs/MFDs. Holding advice meetings or collecting KYC in DIFC without DFSA authorisation, or onshore without a CMA licence, is the clearest red line.
- INFERENCE: Under the UAE finfluencer regime, an Indian adviser running Gulf-targeted YouTube, Instagram or seminar content with recommendations on UAE-regulated products may need a UAE licence. Whether content solely about Indian mutual funds counts as "regulated UAE financial products" is unclear, so treat Gulf-targeted recommendation content as licensable unless counsel advises otherwise.
- INFERENCE: The lack of named actions against SEBI-registered Indian firms likely reflects low visibility and complaint-driven enforcement, not permission. The SEBI-side risk is mainly in client-protection terms (unsuitable products, return claims) and the 2024 association rules.

### Gaps
- No Gulf, MAS or FCA enforcement naming a SEBI-registered Indian RIA, MFD or broker for cross-border solicitation of NRIs was found.
- No CBUAE, Kuwait CMA, Oman FSA, Saudi CMA or Bahrain CBB actions involving Indian advisers were found.
- No SEBI order specifically concerning NRI mis-selling was found. The search returned only general unregistered-IA cases.
- The MAS Investor Alert List could not be searched.

## 5. Common lawful outreach patterns

### Takeaway
The observable lawful patterns are: (1) licensed local presence (DIFC/ADGM) running events and client meetings; (2) JVs or partnerships with locally licensed banks, brokers or exchange houses (Geojit-BBK/Barjeel; Aspora-LuLu Exchange for payments); (3) industry and community platforms (Cafemutual's Dubai summit with AMC CEOs, ICAI Dubai Chapter NRI-tax sessions, AMFI/AMC investor-awareness programmes); (4) media and investor education rather than recommendations; and (5) client-initiated onboarding from India, about to become easier under SEBI's proposed digital PROI KYC.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: Cafemutual Investment Summit 2025 in Dubai (20 Oct 2025) had four AMC CEOs speaking on "Mutual Funds for NRIs" plus sessions on cross-border portfolios and GIFT City. The audience was MFDs and wealth managers — [Cafemutual](https://cafemutual.com/news/events/36246-know-how-nris-can-include-india-in-their-global-portfolios); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/why-now-is-the-moment-for-gulf-nris-to-bet-big-on-indias-equity-markets?amp=1)
- VERIFIED: The ICAI Dubai Chapter hosted an India Union Budget event (29 Jul 2024) covering NRI tax provisions. An ICAI webinar (1 Jan 2025) covered NRI taxation and mutual fund portfolios — [ICAI Dubai newsletter Q2 2024](https://icaidubai.org/ebook/2nd-quarter-2024/files/basic-html/page10.html); [ICAI event](https://www.icai.org/newevent/9344)
- VERIFIED: AMFI publishes a schedule of AMC investor-awareness programmes. Visible entries were India-based, and none were found in the Gulf — [AMFI IAP schedule](https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/awareness-program). PPFAS runs an NRI Centre and investor-awareness programmes — [PPFAS NRI Centre](https://amc.ppfas.com/investor-desk/nri-center/index.php)
- VERIFIED: Aspora (an NRI investing/remittance platform) provides UAE payment services through LuLu International Exchange LLC (CBUAE-regulated), which onboards clients directly — [Aspora](https://www.aspora.com/blog/how-uae-based-nris-can-invest-in-mutual-funds-in-india)
- VERIFIED: Geojit's NRI desk at BBK Bahrain was staffed with Geojit-trained personnel under a 2007 exclusive agreement. The UAE and Kuwait JVs hold local licences — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/303572); [Geojit NRI services](https://www.geojit.com/gil/nri-services)
- VERIFIED: Barjeel Geojit runs a UAE-regulated umbrella fund (Barjeel Geojit India Opportunities Fund), i.e. a locally regulated India-access product — [Geojit](https://geojit.com/nri-services)
- VERIFIED: The Neovision–DSP ADGM fund and The Wealth Company GIFT AIF are product-led models aimed at Gulf NRIs — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/neovision-wealth-management-bring-end-to-end-digital-access-to-indian-equities-via-an-adgm-based-fund-with-dsp-asset-managers-1458491); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-wealth-company-launches-gift-city-fund-to-give-nris-a-single-dollar-denominated-route-into-indias-mutual-fund-market-302851983.html)
- VERIFIED: SEBI's ad code exempts nothing by channel, so social and electronic messaging count as advertisements. The 2024 association rules exempt pure investor education — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/sebi-issues-advertisement-code-for-investment-advisers-research-analysts/); [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2024/09/223-sebi-regulated-firms-barred-from-collaborating-with-unregistered-finfluencers/)
- VERIFIED: UK FPO Art. 31 allows follow-up non-real-time communications to "previously overseas customers". This is relevant to NRIs who were clients while in the Gulf or India and later moved to the UK — [legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1529/article/31/made)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: A defensible playbook for SELEQT, with no foreign licence:
  - (a) India-hosted webinars and content on NRI tax, FEMA, repatriation and GIFT City, framed as education, IA-code compliant and supervisory-body approved, without Gulf-specific product recommendations.
  - (b) Speaking at community or professional-body events (ICAI chapters, Indian business councils, alumni and state associations) as an invited educator, with no fees paid to the host contingent on clients.
  - (c) Clients contacting SELEQT and being onboarded, advised and transacting entirely from India, with records of client initiative. Reverse-solicitation records are fragile under ESMA's view and the post-2023 UAE CMA rules.
  - (d) Referral partnerships only with regulated parties, e.g. Gulf-licensed advisers or AMFI-registered overseas distributors.
  - (e) Upgrading to a DFSA Representative Office or a Category 4 licence once volume justifies it.
- INFERENCE: Exchange houses and banks in the Gulf are CBUAE-licensed for remittance/banking, not investment promotion. Co-marketing Indian MFs through them may itself require the partner's or SELEQT's CMA promotion licence. Payment-rail partnerships (like Aspora-LuLu) are lower risk than product co-promotion.

### Gaps
- No documented case was found of a Gulf exchange house formally co-promoting an Indian AMC's or adviser's products.
- No 2026 Gulf community-hosted NRI investment seminar with a named Indian RIA/MFD speaker was found.
- How Gulf regulators treat in-person education seminars by unlicensed foreign advisers (education vs promotion) is not established by a primary source. The UAE finfluencer licence explicitly mentions in-person events, which suggests risk.
