## Q1. Overseas licensed entities and rep offices (DIFC/DFSA, ADGM, QFC, MAS, FCA, SEC), what they permit, and GIFT IFSC use

### Takeaway
Most large Indian wealth managers have now set up regulated offshore entities. DIFC is the default Gulf hub, usually under a DFSA Category 4 (advise and arrange) or Category 3C licence. 360 ONE (DIFC since 2010, MAS, plus US/Canada presence), Nuvama (DIFC Cat 3C in 2024, MAS) and ASK (DIFC in Nov 2024) are the clearest cases. Anand Rathi is the outlier: it has an unlicensed-sounding "representative office" in mainland Dubai (Business Bay) and, since Dec 2025, an FCA-authorised UK subsidiary. GIFT IFSC is used mainly as a USD product shelf (Cat III AIFs, outbound funds), not as an advisory licence for NRIs. Searches found no Indian wealth firm licensed in ADGM, QFC (Qatar), Saudi CMA, Oman or Kuwait.

### Inferences
- There are three tiers. (1) Full offshore booking or advisory: 360 ONE (DIFC, MAS, US/Canada), Nuvama (DIFC Cat 3C, MAS) and ASK (DIFC Cat 4-type). (2) Licensed rep or liaison presence plus India booking: Anand Rathi (Dubai rep office) and Waterfield (Dubai office, licence unclear). Anand Rathi uses a properly authorised entity only in the UK. (3) India-only booking with digital outreach: fee-only RIAs, Wealth Ladder, 1 Finance, PL, Sanctum.
- DIFC Cat 4 and Cat 3C licences generally restrict firms to Professional Clients (roughly USD 1m net assets), which fits the UHNI positioning of these firms. Mass-affluent Gulf NRIs are therefore served from India under SEBI registration.
- GIFT IFSC is used as a product wrapper (USD AIFs, outbound funds, FDs) that firms then distribute to NRIs. It is not a home-country licence for soliciting abroad.

- Anand Rathi's NRI share of AUM is not disclosed in the search results.

## Q3. Handling foreign marketing rules (disclaimers, client-initiated language, partner-led distribution)

### Takeaway
The regulatory perimeter forces three patterns: get a local licence (DIFC Cat 3C/4, MAS CMS, FCA), sell through a locally licensed partner (UAE SCA/CMA promoter, foreign bank tie-up), or rely on documented reverse solicitation, with the adviser booking from India under SEBI registration. SEBI separately requires anyone advising NRIs or PIOs to be SEBI-registered, so the India-side licence is mandatory either way.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: Under the UAE SCA's 2023 regime (Decisions 02/RM and 04/RM of 2023), only SCA-licensed promoters may actively promote foreign funds onshore. Retail promotion of foreign funds is effectively prohibited. Firms outside the UAE or in free zones may deal only through documented reverse solicitation done cross-border, with the promoter not present onshore. Foreign funds registered with the SCA can be privately placed to professional investors via a licensed promoter, with an AED 500k minimum. — [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/bbb77961/sweeping-changes-to-the-sca-rules-on-the-distribution-of-foreign-funds-in-the-uae); [Morgan Lewis, Mar 2023](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2023/03/scas-official-guidance-on-marketing-of-foreign-funds-in-mainland-uae-five-key-takeaways); [CMS](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/promotion-and-introduction-of-financial-products-in-the-uae-the-new-framework#utm_campaign=redirect_law-now)

## Q4. Regulatory actions abroad or in India related to NRI marketing

### Takeaway
Searches found no DFSA, SCA/CMA, MAS, FCA or SEBI enforcement action against the named Indian wealth firms over NRI marketing. The relevant precedents are DFSA actions against unauthorised activity or promotion by other firms, and SEBI's steady stream of orders against unregistered (mostly Telegram-based) advisers.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: In Oct 2026 the DFSA fined Vault Wealth (an ADGM-licensed wealth manager) Dhs401,000 for providing financial services from the DIFC without DFSA authorisation, as its staff operated from a related DIFC entity's offices in 2024. The fine included a 30% settlement discount. — [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2026/finance/dfsa-fines-vault-wealth-unauthorised-difc-financial-services/); [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/blog/dfsa-fines-vault-wealth-dh401000-for-unauthorised-difc-transactions/)
- VERIFIED: In Nov 2024 the DFSA fined Vedas International Marketing Management USD 100,000 for unauthorised and misleading promotions of Multibank Group in the DIFC. — [Emirates 24|7](https://www.emirates247.com/business/corporate/dfsa-fines-company-for-unauthorised-misleading-financial-promotions-2024-11-05-1.735139); [Gulf Today](https://www.gulftoday.ae/news/2024/11/05/dubai-financial-services-authority-slaps-$100000-fine-on-firm-for-unauthorised)
- VERIFIED: In Mar 2026 SEBI debarred Yash Garg (Yash Trading Academy) for two years and ordered refunds over unregistered advice through Telegram. Other orders hit Safe Trading, Intraday Jackpot and Baap of Chart, where an "educational purposes only" disclaimer did not save the operators. — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/03/223-sebi-unregistered-advisor-telegram-social-media-scrutiny-complaint/); [SEBI order Dec 2024](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/dec-2024/1733151002336_1.pdf); [MSEI circular](https://www.msei.in/SX-Content/Circulars/2025/March/Circular-16940.pdf)

### Inferences
- The Vault Wealth case is the most relevant precedent for Indian firms. Having staff physically working from a free zone where the entity is not authorised, or from mainland UAE without SCA/CMA authorisation, is the enforcement risk. This supports the "licensed entity or rep office plus booking in India" structure.
- The SEBI Baap of Chart reasoning implies that disclaimers alone do not cure unregistered advisory activity. The same logic would apply to unregistered "NRI finfluencers" based abroad who advise NRIs on Indian securities.

### Gaps
- Searches found no SCA/CMA, MAS, FCA or SEC actions involving Indian wealth managers marketing to NRIs.
- Searches found no SEBI order specifically about NRI-targeted marketing by registered intermediaries. A search of SEBI's orders page by entity name was not possible in this environment.
