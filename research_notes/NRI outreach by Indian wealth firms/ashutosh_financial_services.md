## Q4. Target countries and handling of the foreign-regulation question

### Takeaway
The firm targets a wide spread of diaspora markets: the Gulf (UAE and others), the UK, East Africa (Kenya, Uganda), Singapore, Australia, and North America (US, Canada via tax-return content). I found no published text on how it handles overseas securities or advice rules. The site has a "Disclaimer" page, but its wording was not visible in any search result.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: Countries with dedicated content or events: UAE/Gulf (2020), Singapore (2024), UK (2025 twice), Kenya (2025, 2026), Uganda (2026), Australia (2025). — sources as listed in Q3
- VERIFIED: A third-party listing names client countries including Australia, Africa, Canada, New Zealand, U.S.A. and U.A.E. — [IndiaMART](https://www.indiamart.com/ashutosh-ficom/aboutus.html)
- VERIFIED: Content on reporting Indian income in US and Canadian returns, and on US stock investing. — [NRI Services](https://ashutoshfinserv.com/nri-services/); [Foreign Equity](https://ashutoshfinserv.com/nri-services/foreign-equity/)
- VERIFIED: Pages link to a Disclaimer page, but no search result showed its text. — [Company Publication](https://ashutoshfinserv.com/general/)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: The positioning is "education + Indian tax/FEMA compliance + India-domiciled products", delivered from India. Indian tax and FEMA work is generally not a regulated activity in host countries. Advising on or soliciting securities is regulated, notably in the US (SEC/state IA rules), Canada, the UK (FCA financial promotion rules), Singapore (MAS) and the UAE (SCA/DFSA/FSRA). Leading with tax education lowers that risk. I saw no explicit "reverse solicitation" or "only clients who approach us" statement.
- INFERENCE: A US/Canada client of an Indian MFD usually faces AMC-level restrictions (many AMCs do not accept US/Canada NRIs), which may explain why North America content is mostly tax compliance.

### Gaps
- No disclaimer wording, partner arrangements or foreign-licence workarounds found. A manual review of ashutoshfinserv.com/disclaimer from an unrestricted network is recommended.
