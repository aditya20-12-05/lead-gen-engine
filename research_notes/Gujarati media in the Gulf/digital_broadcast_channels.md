# Digital and Broadcast Gujarati Channels Reaching Gulf-Based Gujaratis (UAE, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait, KSA)

Research date: 2026-10-11. Method: about 25 WebSearch queries (standard and extended, plus Gujarati-script queries). WebFetch was tried on e& (eand.ae) and failed with `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`, so no channel-list page was read in full. Every claim below comes from search-result snippets, not from reading the full page. Tags: **VERIFIED** means a search snippet from the cited URL supports the claim. **INFERENCE** means it is my reasoning and has no source. Many search results came from low-quality SEO pages (for example templated "blog" pages on unrelated domains). I dropped those, and I mark them where they matter.

## Key Question 1: Which Gujarati channels reach the Gulf, and which have measurable Gulf audiences (subscriber or member counts, distribution deals, Gulf editions or bureaus)?

### Takeaway
I could not find a Gujarati channel, paper or creator that publishes a Gulf-specific audience figure. The countable audiences are three kinds. First, Gulf pay-TV carriage of a small number of Gujarati channels: Colors Gujarati on e& eLife channel 933 (on the list since at least 2015), and an older du listing for a "Zee News / Gujarati" channel. Second, the global YouTube reach of Gujarati news channels (TV9 Gujarati about 4.8 to 5.0M subscribers, VTV about 2.76M, ABP Asmita about 1.31M, GSTV about 1.17M), which Gulf viewers can watch but which is not broken out by country. Third, community associations with stated memberships in Bahrain, Qatar and Oman. No Gulf edition or Gulf bureau was found for any Gujarati daily. The overseas editions I found are in the UK and US only.

## Key Question 2: Do Gujarati papers in India run NRI pages or columns by financial experts, and how do contributors get published there?

### Takeaway
Gujarat Samachar has an "NRI News" section on its web, app and English site. Sandesh is reported to have an NRI supplement and an international (Chicago) edition. Both are news-oriented and focus on the US, UK, Canada and Australia. I found no evidence of a regular NRI finance column written by an outside financial expert in any of these papers. I found no published contributor or submission guidelines for Gujarat Samachar, Sandesh or Divya Bhaskar.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: Gujarat Samachar has an NRI News section ("Updates for Gujaratis across the globe") and an English NRI category covering the USA, UK, Canada, Australia and more. — [Google Play app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gujaratsamachar&hl=en_US); [English NRI category](https://english.gujaratsamachar.com/category/nri-news/1)
- VERIFIED but low-quality source: a PDF on an unrelated domain lists an "NRI Supplement" among Sandesh's sections. The same search confirmed Sandesh International (Chicago) via Wikipedia. — [Wikipedia: Sandesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandesh_(Indian_newspaper))
- VERIFIED: Divya Bhaskar has a "DB Column" section for well-known Gujarati writers. The late Kanti Bhatt wrote daily columns there. Its app carries market, IPO and gold-rate news. No NRI finance column was found. — [Wikipedia: Divya Bhaskar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divya_Bhaskar); [Wikipedia: Kanti Bhatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanti_Bhatt)
- VERIFIED: A search for Gujarati-language NRI tax or personal-finance columns (Gujarat Samachar, Gujarati Mid-day) found no columnists. It only found CA firms advertising NRI tax services. — e.g. [Dinesh Kanabar, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinesh_Kanabar) (Gujarat-born tax expert; no column confirmed)
- VERIFIED: A US-focused Gujarati weekly, Gujarat Times, lists editor@gujarattimesusa.com as its editorial contact. That is the only Gujarati-outlet submission contact the search returned. — [CUNY community media directory](https://directory-ccm.journalism.cuny.edu/outlet/?id=274)
- VERIFIED: Muck Rack keeps a Divya Bhaskar outlet page listing journalists and contacts. It may be useful for finding editors. — [Muck Rack: Divya Bhaskar](https://muckrack.com/media-outlet/divyabhaskar)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: Contributors usually reach Indian regional dailies by pitching the relevant desk editor directly (business, supplement or NRI desk) by email, or through a PR or media agency, or by placing paid or sponsored "advertorial" content. This is common industry practice. It is not confirmed for these specific papers.
- INFERENCE: A Gulf-focused finance column would be new ground, because the existing NRI sections cover US, UK and Canada news. That could be a pitching angle.
- INFERENCE: Any finance contributor who gives specific investment recommendations in print would need to consider SEBI's investment-adviser and research-analyst rules (see Key Question 3).

### Gaps
- No public submission guidelines or NRI-desk contact emails were found for Gujarat Samachar, Sandesh or Divya Bhaskar. These would need to be checked on each paper's "contact" page, which WebFetch could not reach.
- Not confirmed: whether Sandesh's NRI supplement is still published, and how often.
- Not checked: the business supplements (e.g. Gujarat Samachar "Business Plus" style pages) for named finance columnists.

## Key Question 3: Are there Gujarati-language finance content creators targeting Gulf NRIs, and are they SEBI-registered?

### Takeaway
I could not identify any Gujarati-language finance creator who specifically targets Gulf NRIs. The Gujarati finance video content I found is institutional and educational (Groww Gujarati, SBI Mutual Fund's "Fund Guru Quick Takes in Gujarati"), plus small independent trading channels. SEBI does not register YouTube channels as such. Only individual advisers (RIA) and research analysts (RA) can be registered. I found no Gujarati Gulf-NRI creator with a confirmed SEBI registration.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: **Groww Gujarati** is an educational YouTube channel covering stocks and mutual funds in Gujarati. It states that Groww does not advise on individual stocks. No subscriber count appeared. — [Groww Gujarati YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@GrowwGujarati)
- VERIFIED: SBI Mutual Fund publishes a "Fund Guru Quick Takes in Gujarati" playlist explaining mutual fund concepts. — [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLFJWuLVcgVA7nDiAT-Q9EZ9P2UbatW3Ac)
- VERIFIED: A small "Gujarati trader" channel (intraday and Bank Nifty) shows very low engagement. — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCusMeqslf8N7PNIK67aOqSQ)
- VERIFIED: Brokers publish Gujarati explainers, for example Angel One on SEBI versus AMFI and 5paisa Gujarati news. PersonalFN has a Gujarati article on SEBI's warning to finfluencers. — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/knowledge-center/share-market/difference-between-sebi-and-amfi-gujarati); [5paisa Gujarati](https://www.5paisa.com/gujarati/news/353); [PersonalFN](https://www.personalfn.com/dwl/Mutual-Funds/sebi-chiefs-warning-to-finfluencers-heres-what-she-said-gujarati)
- VERIFIED: SEBI acted against unregistered finfluencers. YouTuber Ravindra Balu Bharti was barred and ordered to refund about Rs 9.5 crore (Dec 2024). — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/explained-youtube-influencer-fined-rs-9-5-cr-for-illegal-investment-advice-124121600507_1.html); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/sebi-action-youtuber-ravindra-bharti-unregistered-investmentadvicepenalties-124121801065_1.html)
- VERIFIED (law-firm opinion): giving stock or fund recommendations on YouTube without SEBI registration is illegal. SEBI has not clearly confirmed whether NRIs can register as investment advisers. — [Vikas Gupta: finfluencer legal risks](https://lawyervikasgupta.com/blog/legal-risks-of-running-a-finfluencer-youtube-channel/); [Can NRIs become SEBI RIAs?](https://lawyervikasgupta.com/blog/can-nris-offer-investment-advice-in-india/)
- VERIFIED: SEBI's investor helpline is available in eight languages, including Gujarati. — [SEBI toll-free helpline](https://www.sebi.gov.in/toll-free-helpline.html)
- VERIFIED: Gujarati news channels (GSTV, ABP Asmita, VTV) were the only large Gujarati YouTube presences the finance searches returned. None was identified as running an NRI finance show. — [GSTV, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GSTV)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: There appears to be a gap in the market for Gujarati-language, Gulf-NRI-focused personal-finance content (NRE/NRO accounts, FEMA, repatriation, GIFT City, India tax for UAE residents). No identifiable creator holds this space in search results, but search-engine coverage of YouTube and Instagram is weak, so smaller creators may exist.
- INFERENCE: To check any creator found later, search the SEBI intermediary registers (Investment Advisers or Research Analysts) for their name or registration number. A channel itself cannot be "SEBI-registered".

### Gaps
- No Gulf-based Gujarati finance creator was found, nor any Gujarati finance creator whose content explicitly targets the UAE or GCC. Subscriber counts for Groww Gujarati were not visible.
- SEBI register lookups were not possible (WebFetch blocked). Registration status of any Gujarati creator is unverified.
- No Gulf-based Gujarati lifestyle YouTube or Instagram creators with large followings were found. The only Gulf-Gujarati Instagram account surfaced is @ame_gujarati_in_uae (about 6K followers).
