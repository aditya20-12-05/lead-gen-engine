# Gujarati-language and Gujarati-serving media in Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait and Saudi Arabia

Research date: 11 October 2026. Method note: WebFetch failed in this container on every host tried (for example `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND` for bi50.bahrainthismonth.com and www.gsq.org.in). So every finding below comes from web-search result snippets and the search engine's summaries of indexed pages, not from reading the full pages. Tags: **VERIFIED** means a search result tied the claim to the URL cited. **INFERENCE** means it is my reasoning. When a page's date could not be confirmed, I say so.

Bottom line: I found **no Gujarati-language newspaper, magazine, radio show, TV channel or news site based in any of the five countries, today or in the past**. Gujarati audiences there are reached through (a) Gujarati associations' Facebook, YouTube and WhatsApp channels, (b) English-language dailies' community pages, and (c) Hindi FM radio. Gujarati associations are well documented in Qatar, Oman and Bahrain. They are only weakly documented, or informal, in Kuwait and Saudi Arabia.

## Key Question 1: Country-by-country inventory of Gujarati and Gujarati-serving outlets (name, city, format, frequency, language, reach, owner, 2025-26 activity, contact)

### Takeaway
Each country's "Gujarati media" is really its Gujarati association's own channels (Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp, events), plus English-language dailies and Hindi radio. Only Gujarati Samaj Qatar showed confirmed 2025-26 activity: a 2025-26 committee on its website and a Sept-Oct 2025 Navratri fiesta. Muscat Gujarati Samaj's latest dated press coverage is April 2022. Bahrain's Samaj is profiled in a 2026-dated Bahrain This Month feature. Kuwait and Saudi Arabia have no verified formal Gujarati association with a web presence.

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: In all five countries, the most practical "Gujarati media" for reaching Gujarati readers are the associations' WhatsApp broadcast lists and Facebook/YouTube pages, plus sponsorship of their festival events (Navratri, Diwali, Gujarat Day, kite festivals). Circulation or reach figures for these channels are not published.
- INFERENCE: Of the mainstream outlets, the English dailies with demonstrated Gujarati Samaj coverage are Gulf Times (Qatar), Muscat Daily, Oman Daily Observer and Times of Oman (Oman), and GDN, Gulf Weekly and Bahrain This Month (Bahrain). They are the most evidence-backed routes to Gujarati readers.
- INFERENCE: Hindi FM (Radio Olive and One FM in Qatar, Mirchi in Bahrain, the Radio Kuwait Hindi hour, possibly Capital Radio Hindi in Saudi Arabia) reaches Gujarati listeners as part of a broader North-Indian audience. None has a Gujarati-specific show that I could find.

## Key Question 2: Does any Gujarati-language newspaper or magazine exist in these countries today or historically (for example among Muscat's old Gujarati merchant community)?

### Takeaway
Searches in English and in Gujarati script found no current or historical Gujarati-language newspaper or magazine published in Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait or Saudi Arabia. Muscat's Gujarati merchant heritage survives in **archival documents** (account books and letters in Gujarati), not in a local press.

## Key Question 4: Do Indian embassies list community media?

### Takeaway
None of the five Indian missions was found to publish a list of community media outlets. The embassies do publish **association registries or guidelines** (Kuwait), **their own newsletters** (Kuwait's monthly India-Kuwait Media Connect, Muscat's newsletter page), and **community pages** (Bahrain). In Qatar, Gujarati Samaj works under the embassy-backed Indian Cultural Centre (ICC) umbrella.

### Cited Findings
- **Kuwait**: the embassy registers Indian associations on request. Nearly 210 are registered, and registration is "only an acknowledgement of their presence". New associations write to community[dot]kuwait[at]mea[dot]gov[dot]in. VERIFIED — [Embassy of India Kuwait: association guidelines](https://indembkwt.gov.in/association-guidelines.php)
- **Kuwait**: the embassy launched a **digital monthly "India-Kuwait Media Connect" newsletter** and a monthly informal journalists' meeting under Ambassador Paramita Tripathi. VERIFIED — [Kuwait Times: India-Kuwait Media Connect](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/50805/kuwait/other-news/india-kuwait-media-connect-opens-platform-for-dialogue-cooperation/). Earlier: India Business Bulletin (2021) — [Embassy PDF](https://www.indembkwt.gov.in/pdf/Press%20Release%20-%20%20INDIA%20BUSINESS%20BULLETIN.pdf); Indian Cultural Network — [Embassy PDF](https://www.indembkwt.gov.in/pdf/ICN2(1).pdf)
- **Oman**: the Embassy of India Muscat has a "news letter" section and event archives that name Muscat Gujarati Samaj. Its Community Welfare Wing has toll-free number 80071234 (per search summary; may be dated). VERIFIED — [Embassy Muscat news letter](https://www.indemb-oman.gov.in/news_letter); [Embassy Muscat archive](https://www.indemb-oman.gov.in/archive/events-photo-gallery/20/8/)
- **Bahrain**: the Embassy of India Bahrain has an "Indian community" page. VERIFIED — [eoibahrain.gov.in: Indian community](https://eoibahrain.gov.in/indian-community.php)
- **Qatar**: the ICC works "under the aegis of the Embassy of India" with affiliated associations. The embassy, through the ICC, ordered a Goan association renamed and re-registered in 2025, which shows its oversight. VERIFIED — [Peninsula, Feb 2024](https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/19/02/2024/25-indian-associations-in-qatar-perform-for-dance-festival); [The Goan](https://www.thegoan.net/global-goenkar/goan-association-in-qatar-to-get-new-identity-committee/134127.html). MEA's list of overseas Indian associations includes ICC contacts. VERIFIED — [MEA PDF](https://www.mea.gov.in/images/pdf/list-of-overseas-indian.pdf)
- **Saudi Arabia**: no embassy (Riyadh) or consulate (Jeddah) community-media or association listing surfaced. GAP

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: The embassies' association registries (Kuwait), ICC affiliate lists (Qatar) and ISC wing structures (Oman) are the most reliable way to confirm a Gujarati body exists. The embassy community wings are also a channel for verifying contacts.
- INFERENCE: Kuwait's Media Connect newsletter and journalists' meetings mean the embassy keeps a working list of Indian-community journalists, but it is not public.

### Gaps
- I could not open the embassy pages to check for a downloadable association list naming any Gujarati body, especially in Kuwait.
- I found no evidence that any mission (Doha, Muscat, Manama, Kuwait, Riyadh/Jeddah) publishes a community-media directory.
