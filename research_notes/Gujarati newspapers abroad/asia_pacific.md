## Q1. Outlet inventory: name, city, publisher, language, frequency, format, reach, active in 2026, business/finance content, contact route

### Takeaway
The only dedicated Gujarati-language outlets with real reach in this region are in Australia:
- SBS Gujarati (public broadcaster; radio, podcast and web; clearly active in 2026). It is the strongest single channel.
- Namaskar Gujarat (Brisbane-based print/e-paper; calls itself "Australia's only Gujarati newspaper"; last confirmed issues 2023 and a July 2025 web headline).
- Two smaller or possibly dormant titles: Gujarat Times (Sydney) and Satya Times (Melbourne).

Elsewhere (NZ, Singapore, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Fiji, Kobe) I found no Gujarati-language newspaper. Gujarati readers there are reached through community associations or English-language Indian diaspora papers. One exception is Namaste Bharat Kiwi Times in NZ, which carries some Gujarati content.

## Q3. Regulatory note for a foreign (India-based) adviser placing finance columns or ads (Australia ASIC/AFSL; Singapore MAS)

### Takeaway
In both countries, advertising aimed at residents is the step most likely to trigger licensing. In Australia, the overseas-provider exemption (s911A(2A) as modified by reg 7.6.02AG) applies only if the provider does not engage in conduct intended or likely to induce people in Australia to use the service. Singapore's MAS regulates financial advertisements under the FAR (Financial Advisers Regulations) and SFLCBR (Securities and Futures (Licensing and Conduct of Business) Regulations), and in May 2025 proposed removing most exclusions. Legal advice is needed before running ads in either market.

### Cited Findings
- ASIC: the s911A(2A) exemption (as modified by reg 7.6.02AG, Corporations Regulations 2001) applies only if the overseas provider "does not engage in conduct that is intended to induce people in Australia to use the service or is likely to have that effect". In 2018 ASIC granted unique relief to an Ontario-regulated adviser serving clients who had moved to Australia. — [IFA](https://www.ifa.com.au/news/26271-asic-makes-unique-afsl-decision-on-foreign-adviser); [Public Accountant](https://publicaccountant.com.au/news/asic-makes-unique-afsl-decision-foreign-adviser/)
- Reverse-solicitation pitfalls for foreign providers are discussed by an Australian law firm. — [Thomson Geer](https://www.thomsons.com.au/insights/reversing-into-a-regulator-reverse-solicitation-pitfalls)
- MAS: in May 2025 MAS proposed removing the exclusions from the financial advertisement rules in the FAR and SFLCBR. The only remaining exception would be dissemination required by law, a court or listing rules. Consultation closed 5 June 2025, and the final outcome was not found. — [Rajah & Tann](https://www.rajahtannasia.com/viewpoints/mas-proposes-to-remove-exclusions-exempting-fis-from-complying-with-financial-advertisement-regulations-to-better-protect-consumers/); [Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/banking-finance_1/singapore-mas-consults-on-proposed-revisions-to-remove-exemptions-under-the-financial-advertisement-regulations)
- MAS: Reg 32CB allows an ASEAN-country entity to give advice on ASEAN-listed capital markets products without a licence under an approved arrangement with a Singapore entity (from 1 Jan 2019). India is not ASEAN. — [Allen & Gledhill](https://www.allenandgledhill.com/sg/publication/articles/8727/new-exemption-in-financial-advisers-regulations-for-entities-from-asean-participating-countries-providing-financial-advisory-service-under-approved-arrangements)

### Inferences
- Paid ads or sponsored finance columns in SBS-adjacent, Namaskar Gujarat, Indian Link or Tabla! media, aimed at residents, would likely count as "inducing" conduct in Australia and as regulated advertising in Singapore.
- Educational, unpromoted expert commentary (e.g. on Indian tax or FEMA for NRIs) carries lower risk, but it is still a legal question.
- Content focused on India-side products for NRIs may also engage SEBI/RBI rules (out of scope).

### Gaps
- No ASIC regulatory guide specifically on foreign advisers' print advertising was found in search.
- The final status of MAS's 2025 advertising consultation as of October 2026 was not found.
