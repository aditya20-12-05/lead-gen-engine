# Gujarati-language and bilingual newspapers and magazines in the USA and Canada (Gujarati diaspora)

Research date: 2026-10-11. Method: WebSearch only (standard and extended, plus one Gujarati-script query). WebFetch failed with DNS errors (ENOTFOUND) on every host tried (directory-ccm.journalism.cuny.edu, gujaratabroad.ca), so no publisher page was read in full. All facts come from search-result summaries of the cited pages. Tags: **VERIFIED** means a cited URL supports the claim as reported in search snippets. **INFERENCE** means analyst judgement. Several "guide" pages in the results (clcloud.codelathe.com, rolci.desarrollo.edu.py, dev2.expipoint.com, npm.vehikl.com and similar) looked like auto-generated SEO spam on hijacked subdomains. They were **excluded** as sources.

## Inventory: name, city, publisher, language, frequency, format, reach, 2026 status, finance pages, contact route

### Takeaway
The North American Gujarati press is small and made up mostly of weeklies. The core US titles are Gujarat Times (NY/NJ, Parikh Worldwide Media), Garavi Gujarat USA (Asian Media Group USA, whose parent is in London) and the US weekly editions of the Indian dailies Gujarat Samachar (NY), Sandesh (Chicago) and Divya Bhaskar (NJ/NY). Canada's titles are free GTA weeklies: Gujarat Abroad, Gujarat Weekly and Gujarat Newsline. Audited circulation figures barely exist. Every number found is self-reported or more than ten years old.

### Cited Findings

   - VERIFIED (self-reported claim): "one of the largest Gujarati newspapers in the U.S." and "market leader in terms of both the readership, subscription and advertising revenues in Gujarati weeklies in USA". No figure is given — [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/c/gujarat-times/348441780)
   - VERIFIED: Sister titles under Parikh Worldwide Media are News India Times (national weekly), Desi Talk New York, Desi Talk Chicago and The Indian American magazine. The CUNY directory calls the group "the largest Indian-American publishing group in the U.S." and says Gujarat Times has "nationwide circulation" — [CUNY CCM directory, Parikh](https://directory-ccm.journalism.cuny.edu/outlet/?id=29); [CUNY CCM, Gujarat Times](https://directory-ccm.journalism.cuny.edu/outlet/?id=274)

   - VERIFIED: Revenue is estimated at under US$5M with 11–50 employees. This is an aggregator estimate, not audited — [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/c/gujarat-times/348441780)
   - VERIFIED: Organisational contacts listed by CUNY are website gujarattimesusa.com, phone (212) 675-7515, and editor@gujarattimesusa.com. The CUNY entry is undated — [CUNY CCM](https://directory-ccm.journalism.cuny.edu/outlet/?id=274)

It covers business, sport, Bollywood, fashion and health, and "columnists" — [PressReader listing](https://www.pressreader.com/newspapers/n/garavi-gujarat-usa); [PressReader issue 2024-04-13](https://www.pressreader.com/newspapers/n/garavi-gujarat-usa/20240413)

## Which outlets have the largest affluent readership?

### Takeaway
Garavi Gujarat USA and its sister B2B title Asian Hospitality explicitly target Gujarati hotel owners and retail business owners, who form the most clearly affluent, business-owning segment. Gujarat Times reaches a national subscriber base through Parikh Worldwide Media.

### Cited Findings
- Garavi Gujarat USA is "widely read by both professionals and business owners who dominate the motel and retail sectors in the US" — [PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/newspapers/n/garavi-gujarat-usa)
- Asian Hospitality says Indian American hoteliers own "more than half of the hotels in America". The awards coverage says Asian American hoteliers own more than 60% of US hotels and motels. These are different framings of the same point — [About](https://asianhospitality.com/about-us); [Awards 2026](https://www.asianhospitality.com/asian-hospitality-awards-2026-may-12/)
- Asian Hospitality has 10,000+ print subscribers (BPA-certified per its 2021 kit) and 50–55k monthly readers — [2021 kit](https://www.asianhospitality.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/1/2021/02/2021_AsianHosp_Print-Media-Kit-New.pdf)
- Gujarat Times is a "nationally subscribed" weekly, and the parent group is the "largest Indian-American publishing group" — [CUNY CCM](https://directory-ccm.journalism.cuny.edu/outlet/?id=29); [Exact Connections](https://us.exactconnections.com/media/gujarati-times)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: For high-net-worth Gujarati owners and entrepreneurs, the strongest titles are Asian Hospitality and Garavi Gujarat USA, followed by Gujarat Times and News India Times. Canada's free GTA weeklies are mass-reach rather than affluent-targeted.
- INFERENCE: JAINA (Jain Digest) reaches a professionally affluent community (14,000+ households historically), but it is a religious publication and probably carries little commercial advertising.

### Gaps
- No outlet publishes reader income or net-worth demographics that could be found.
- No named business, finance or NRI-money columns were found for any North American Gujarati title. Garavi Gujarat USA lists "business" coverage and "columnists" only in general terms.

## Closures, mergers and product-fit (FATCA/PFIC)

### Takeaway
No 2024–2026 closures or mergers of North American Gujarati titles were found. The only documented closures are 1980s Chicago monthlies. On product fit, several Indian AMCs have restricted US and Canadian investors since about 2015 because of FATCA. US investors also face PFIC taxation on Indian mutual funds, and Canada has no PFIC regime.

### Cited Findings
- No report of a US Gujarati weekly closing or merging turned up in extended search. Historical stoppages were Hindustan Patrika (1977–81), Gujarat Vartaman and Bharat Sandesh (1980s) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Media_in_Gujarati_language)
- Jain Digest stopped print and went online-only in July 2019 — [JAINA](https://www.jaina.org/page/Jain_Digest_Committee)
- FATCA led AMCs including HDFC, ICICI Prudential and Quantum to bar some schemes to US and Canadian residents (2015) — [Business Standard 2015](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/pti-stories/fatca-leads-mfs-to-avoid-fresh-investments-from-us-investors-115011100152_1.html)
- More than 10 AMCs (for example SBI, ICICI Prudential, Nippon India) accept US and Canadian NRIs, often with conditions such as in-person or offline paperwork. This comes from a broker guide — [Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/varsity/can-us-canada-nris-invest-in-indian-mutual-funds)
- The IRS treats Indian mutual funds as PFICs, which requires Form 8621 per fund and can mean tax on unrealised gains. ELSS lock-in does not change this. Canada has no PFIC regime but T1135 applies above CAD 100k — [Taxes for Expats 2026](https://www.taxesforexpats.com/articles/investments/us-tax-on-nri-mutual-funds-india.html); [Scripbox](https://scripbox.com/nri/nri-investment-in-india-from-usa/)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: Any Indian investment product promoted in these outlets has to filter for AMCs and schemes that accept US and Canadian investors, and needs PFIC disclosures for US readers. Canadian Gujarati readers face fewer tax frictions, which may make Canadian outlets a relatively better product fit.

### Gaps
- Whether Gujarat Samachar or Sandesh have quietly suspended their US print editions cannot be confirmed without access to their sites.
